Everything a small shop needs at the counter

Five everyday jobs, handled in one app.

  • Quick checkout

    Add products to a sale and check out in a few taps from the Point of Sale screen.

  • Inventory & low stock

    Manage your products and stock levels, and see at a glance what is out of stock.

  • Customers & balances

    Keep a customer list, track how much each customer owes, and record debt settlements.

  • Invoices & receipts

    View billing history and print receipts for your sales.

  • Business reports

    Log daily expenses and review revenue and profit reports.

  • Also supported

    • Barcode scanning
    • Offline-first: works without an internet connection
