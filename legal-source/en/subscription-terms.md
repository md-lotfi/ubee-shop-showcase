## Manage, cancel and restore

Manage or cancel a Google Play subscription in the [Google Play subscriptions center](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Uninstalling UBee does not cancel it. Cancellation usually stops future renewal while access continues until the paid period ends; refund eligibility is determined by Google Play and applicable law. For a refund request, follow Google Play’s process.

Use UBee’s restore-purchase option to check eligible purchases associated with your store account. Internet access and the same Google Play account may be required. Purchases and entitlements are separate from local shop data: restoring a purchase does not restore invoices, products or backups, and restoring a backup does not itself restore a purchase. RevenueCat supports entitlement validation and purchase restoration; [its privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) explains its service processing.
